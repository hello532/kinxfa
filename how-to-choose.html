How to choose a linear module

Most sizing mistakes happen before the CAD work: the four numbers below select the drive, the body and the seal. Send them to us and you get a drawing and a price back — we do the rest.

01 / FOUR NUMBERS

The four numbers that select a module

Send these four and we return a drawing and a price. Nothing else is needed for a first pass.

  • StrokeUsable travel in mm — e.g. 500 mm
  • Load & orientationPayload in kg, and whether the module runs horizontal or vertical
  • SpeedTravel speed needed in mm/s — e.g. 300 mm/s
  • AccuracyRepeat accuracy you need in mm — e.g. ±0.01 mm
  • EnvironmentDust, metal chips, glue drips or corrosion on site. This decides open, covered or bellows-sealed
  • Drive preferenceBall screw for accuracy, belt for long fast travel, or let us pick
  • Duty cycleCycles per minute and hours per day, so we size the screw and bearings

Speed budget: linear speed = motor rpm / 60 × screw lead. Tell us the speed you need and we pick the lead and the motor together, so the number is met by the build and not hoped for.

02 / DRIVE

Ball screw or belt

Ball screw linear modules

Ball screw and nut

Positioning accuracy comes first

  • Positioning accuracy ±0.01 mm, repeat accuracy 0.003–0.01 mm
  • Motor mounts on the left, right, top or bottom
  • Horizontal or vertical mounting

Belt driven linear modules and slides

Toothed belt

Long, fast moves

  • Fully enclosed and semi-enclosed versions
  • Long-travel transfer at higher speed than a screw drive
  • Horizontal or vertical mounting

Slide table & linear stage modules

Embedded ball screw

A low profile for a tight machine envelope

  • The screw sits inside the body, so the module stays low
  • Proven 500 mm stroke at ±0.01 mm accuracy
  • Repeat accuracy 0.003–0.01 mm

A ball screw at long travel will whip; a belt at short travel wastes the accuracy you paid for. We tell you in the quote which one your numbers actually favour.

03 / ENVIRONMENT

Sealing for your line

The seal is chosen by what is in the air, not by price.

  • With cover (standard)
  • Without cover
  • Bellows-sealed

Tell us dust, metal chips, glue splatter or corrosion risk and the seal choice is part of the quote, not an upsell.

What we will not claim

  • We do not supply original-brand packaging or brand authorisation papers.
  • KK modules are built to match the mounting dimensions and combined height of the equivalent sizes from THK, PMI, MISUMI and others listed on our KK series page. We still ask for your part number and drawing and confirm dimensions with you before you commit.
  • Long rails over 1.5 m are jointed — we state the joint position up front.
  • We are not a semiconductor-lithography class supplier.

04 / PROOF

Applications already shipped

  • Vision labeling machines
  • Medical equipment

Both are from our own published channel footage, not a market segment list. We will not invent industries we have not supplied.
