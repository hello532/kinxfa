KK60 linear module

KK60 is the mainstream size of the KK series. Three models in production, with stroke built to any length from 20 to 810 mm and bodies stocked in 50 mm travel steps. C-grade and P-grade accuracy, servos up to 400 W.

Key takeaways

  • Three models in production: KK6005, KK6010, KK6020.
  • Stroke 20–810 mm built to any length in range; bodies stocked in 50 mm travel steps.
  • C-grade and P-grade accuracy; P-grade repeat accuracy reaches ±0.003 mm.
  • Servos 50–400 W; steppers 42 / 57 / 60.

How this size is built

  • More internal screw-thread recirculation turns than same-size equivalents (e.g. 3.8 turns on KK6005 / KK8610) — higher load capacity, lower noise, tight preload
  • Slider machined and through-hardened from solid bearing steel
  • One-piece slider with ground internal raceway for smoother, quieter running
  • End-cap internal recirculation (Bosch Rexroth ear-type structure) for quieter motion
  • Base locally high-frequency hardened — hard raceway, tough body, easy to modify (extra pin holes, custom length)
  • Base phosphated (0.005–0.008 mm deep, 32–38 HRC, 48 h salt-spray) for better corrosion resistance than blackening
  • Doubled aluminium cover thickness — 6–8 dB lower noise on the same build; covers colour-matched to the base

What we will not claim

  • We do not supply original-brand packaging or brand authorisation papers.
  • KK modules are built to match the mounting dimensions and combined height of the equivalent sizes from THK, PMI, MISUMI and others listed on our KK series page. We still ask for your part number and drawing and confirm dimensions with you before you commit.
  • Long rails over 1.5 m are jointed — we state the joint position up front.
  • We are not a semiconductor-lithography class supplier.
