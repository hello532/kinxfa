Modules we have shipped into live machines

Two jobs are on record: a vision labeling machine and a medical equipment feed. They are not a market list — they are work we have shipped, and the module that went into each one is the same single-axis module you can order.

01 / SHIPPED WORK

Two machines, two modules

Vision labeling machine

KK60 × 2 (X/Y)

Label placement held to a ±0.01 mm positioning class with 0.003–0.01 mm repeatability, so registration stays tight run after run. Two ball-screw axes, sized for the head weight and the cycle rate.

Medical equipment feed

Compact ball-screw stage

A tight enclosure, a clean environment and a short stroke — the axis is sized for the moving mass and the duty cycle, and built enclosed so it stays clean. Positioning class ±0.01 mm.

What we need to size a module for your machine

  • Stroke and the envelope it has to fit in
  • Moving mass and orientation — horizontal or vertical
  • Cycle rate and hours per day
  • Positioning and repeat accuracy the process needs
  • Environment: chips, glue, dust, corrosion, cleanroom

Where these two run

A vision labeling machine keeps its registration from the axis: two ball-screw axes working as X and Y, sized so the head carries at speed without giving up repeatability.

A medical equipment feed is the opposite constraint — a short stroke in a tight, clean envelope, where the axis is chosen for the moving mass and kept enclosed.

Questions about applications
