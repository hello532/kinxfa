Request a quote

We quote by message, not by shopping cart. Send the four items below and we come back with a drawing and a price.

Send us these details

  1. StrokeUsable travel in mm — e.g. 500 mm
  2. Load & orientationPayload in kg, and whether the module runs horizontal or vertical
  3. SpeedTravel speed needed in mm/s — e.g. 300 mm/s
  4. AccuracyRepeat accuracy you need in mm — e.g. ±0.01 mm
  5. EnvironmentDust, metal chips, glue drips or corrosion on site. This decides open, covered or bellows-sealed
  6. Drive preferenceBall screw for accuracy, belt for long fast travel, or let us pick
  7. Duty cycleCycles per minute and hours per day, so we size the screw and bearings

With those we can usually quote without a second round of questions. Design solutions in 6 hours; delivery as little as 24 hours on stock-based builds.
